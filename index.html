What We Paint

1

Interiors

Walls, ceilings, trim and doors. Furniture moved and covered, nail holes filled, edges cut by hand. Most rooms done in a day, most houses in a week.

2

Exteriors

Pressure wash, scrape, sand, prime, caulk, then paint. The prep is half the job and it's always in the estimate. Stucco, siding, brick and trim.

3

Cabinets

Sprayed, not brushed. Doors come off, get finished in a booth, and go back on with the hinges adjusted. Looks like new cabinets for a fifth of the price.

4

Decks, Fences & Property Care

Stain and seal, fence refresh, and the annual touch-ups that keep a house looking cared for. Ask about a yearly plan.
